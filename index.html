Profile & Background

Driven by engineering excellence & thoughtful user experience

A glimpse into my academic trajectory, design philosophy, and core foundations.

I am a third-year undergraduate student pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science with a dual focus on Artificial Intelligence and Human-Computer Interaction. My fascination with software began with a fundamental question: How can we engineer complex systems so intuitive that technology dissolves into the background?

Over the past three years, I have engineered distributed real-time platforms, optimized deep learning retrieval pipelines, and designed design systems adopted by hundreds of collegiate developers. Whether I am tuning database queries in PostgreSQL or fine-tuning micro-interactions in Figma and Tailwind CSS, I operate with an uncompromising standard of craft.

Outside of academics, I lead technical workshops as President of our Web Development Society and contribute to open-source developer tooling.
